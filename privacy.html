Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Preggrs is a pregnancy information app. This policy explains exactly what it

collects, what it does not, and why.

Pregnancy data is among the most sensitive information there is about a person,

and in some jurisdictions carries legal as well as personal risk. The app is

built so that most of it never leaves your device at all.

What stays only on your device

Unless you choose to invite someone, all of the following is stored on your

phone and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Your first name, if you give one, and anything you call the bump
  • Whether you have chosen to record the baby's sex
  • Your estimated due date and how it was worked out
  • The circumstances you selected (for example twins, RhD negative, IVF)
  • Which reminders you have switched on
  • Anything you have read or searched for

The guidance library — foods, cosmetic ingredients, exercise, weekly cards,

appointment timings and warning signs — is compiled into the app itself.

Searching it sends nothing anywhere. It works in aeroplane mode. Scanning a

barcode is the one exception, and it is described below.

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. No

information about your pregnancy is sent to a server in order for a reminder to

appear.

Scanning a barcode

If you scan a product, Preggrs sends the barcode number only to Open Food

Facts (or Open Beauty Facts), a public, non-profit product database, to look up

the product's name and ingredient list.

That request contains nothing else. No account, no due date, no identifier, and

nothing about your pregnancy. We do not keep a record of what you scanned.

The camera is active only while the scanning screen is open. No photo or video

is captured, stored or transmitted — the barcode is decoded on your phone and

the image is discarded.

If you never scan anything, this never happens.

What is stored on a server, and only if you ask

If you create an account and invite a partner or family member, we store the

minimum needed to share:

  • Your email address, for sign-in
  • Your due date, region, circumstances and any nickname you set
  • Notes you or your partner write in the shared journal
  • Which appointments have been marked done
  • The invite codes you generate

Access is enforced at the database level. Someone can only read data for a

pregnancy they have been explicitly invited to, using a single-use code that

expires after 72 hours.

We do not collect mental-health data at all

Preggrs does not administer a mood questionnaire, does not calculate a score,

and does not keep a record of how you are feeling. The "How you're doing"

screen is a signpost to real support — nothing on it is stored, scored or

transmitted, and there is no table in our database that could hold it.

What we never do

  • We do not show advertising, and there are no advertising SDKs in the app.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with data brokers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics that identify you.
  • We do not track your location at all. Preggrs follows US guidance because

that is the only market it is published in, not because it detects where you

are.

  • We do not build profiles of you or use your data to train models.

Your choices

  • Export everything. Settings → Your pregnancy → Export everything writes a

readable JSON file containing everything the app holds about you.

  • Delete everything. Settings → Your pregnancy → Delete everything removes

all local data immediately and permanently.

  • Delete your account. If you created one, deleting your account removes

your profile, your pregnancy record, your invites and your journal entries

from our servers. Email the address below and it will be done within 30 days.

  • Pause. You can stop all reminders and the weekly countdown at any time,

for any reason, without deleting anything.

Children

Preggrs is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect

data from them.

Data retention

Local data persists until you delete it or remove the app. Server data, if you

created an account, persists until you delete your account.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will tell you before the change

takes effect.

Contact

Questions, or to request account deletion: privacy@preggrs.app

Preggrs is an information tool. It does not give medical advice, does not diagnose, and does not assess you. Always follow your own provider.
