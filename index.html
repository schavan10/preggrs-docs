Preggrs

A pregnancy reference app that shows its work. Every claim carries a strength-of-evidence grade and a citation to published US guidance you can open and read yourself.

Preggrs does not give medical advice, and does not assess you. It gathers guidance already published by ACOG, the CDC, the FDA and MotherToBaby, shows how strong the evidence behind each statement is, and links you to the original. It administers no questionnaire, computes no diagnosis, and never tells you a symptom can wait.

What it does

  • Check anything. Search a food, a skincare ingredient, an exercise or an old wives' tale, or scan a barcode. Every answer is graded and cited — and every "avoid" answers the question people actually have, which is what to do now that you have already had it.
  • Week by week. What is happening to the baby, what is happening to you, and the one or two things worth doing — plus a daily tip scoped to the week you are in.
  • Your appointment timeline. Worked out from your due date, with reminders scheduled on your own device.
  • Warning signs. The published list from ACOG and the NHS, in full and unranked, always one tap away. Every line ends in the same instruction: contact a person.
  • Zen. A daily affirmation, a breath pacer, and six sound beds that work offline.

Private by design

The whole guidance library lives inside the app and works in aeroplane mode. No advertising, no ad SDKs, no data brokers, no location tracking. You can export or delete everything at any time. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored and the one case in which anything leaves your phone.

Availability

Preggrs is built for iPhone and published in the United States, following guidance from ACOG, the CDC and the FDA.

Preggrs is an information tool. It does not give medical advice, does not diagnose, and does not assess you. Always follow your own provider.
