Where this app applies

Preggrs is published in the United States and follows guidance from ACOG, the

CDC and the FDA. Where it notes that another authority takes a different view,

that is context, not an alternative to follow. If you are receiving maternity

care outside the US, this app is not built for your care pathway and the

emergency numbers in it will not work.

Preggrs is not medical advice

This is the most important term here.

Preggrs does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or tell you what to do about your

pregnancy. It collects guidance already published by health authorities —

including ACOG, the CDC, the FDA and MotherToBaby — shows you how

strong the evidence behind each statement is, and links you to the original

source so you can read it yourself.

It is an information tool. It is not a medical device, and it is not a substitute

for care from a qualified professional who knows your history.

Preggrs does not assess you. It administers no clinical questionnaire, computes

no score, and does not rank symptoms by how urgent it believes they are. Where

it lists warning signs, it reproduces a published list and tells you to contact

your maternity team — it never tells you that something can wait.

**If anything in this app conflicts with what your midwife, doctor, nurse or

pharmacist has told you about your pregnancy, follow them.**

In an emergency

Do not use an app. Contact your maternity unit, your provider, or emergency

services. The app's urgent-symptoms screen exists to help you decide whether to

call — never to reassure you that you do not need to.

Accuracy and limitations

We work hard to cite accurately and to say plainly how strong each piece of

evidence is. Even so:

  • Guidance changes, and an authority may update its position after we have

cited it.

  • The content is not exhaustive. The absence of something from the app is not a

statement that it is safe.

  • Content in this version has not been reviewed by a named clinician.

Acceptable use

Do not use Preggrs to give medical advice to other people, and do not rely on

the shared family features to communicate anything urgent — they are not

monitored and messages are not delivered in real time.

Accounts and sharing

You are responsible for who you invite. An invite code grants read access to

your pregnancy information; treat it like a password. Codes expire after 72

hours and can only be used once.

Availability

The app is provided as-is. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability of

the sharing features. The core guidance library works offline and does not

depend on our servers.

Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, we are not liable for decisions made on

the basis of information in this app. Your care is between you and your

providers.
