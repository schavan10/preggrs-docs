Support

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Questions, problems, or anything about your data.

This is not a medical service. Preggrs cannot answer questions about your pregnancy or tell you whether a symptom matters. If something is worrying you, contact your maternity unit, midwife or doctor. In an emergency, call 911.

Getting in touch

Email hello@preggrs.app. For anything about your data, including deleting your account, email privacy@preggrs.app.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the Family tab and choose Delete my account. That removes your account, your pregnancy record, everyone you invited and every shared note, permanently. To clear the data held on the phone itself, use Settings → Your pregnancy → Delete everything.

Do I need an account?

No. Every guidance feature — search, scanning, the weekly cards, the appointment timeline, warning signs and the Zen tools — works without signing in. An account is only needed to share your pregnancy with a partner or family member.

Why does the app ask for my camera?

Only to read a barcode on food or skincare packaging. The camera runs while that screen is open, no image is captured or stored, and only the barcode number is sent anywhere.

Why does it not give me a safe/unsafe answer for everything?

Because published guidance does not always give one. Where the evidence is thin, the app says so and grades it accordingly, rather than inventing certainty.

I found something that looks wrong

Please tell us. Every entry in the app cites a source; if a citation is out of date or a claim has been misread, email hello@preggrs.app with the entry name and we will check it.

Preggrs is an information tool. It does not give medical advice, does not diagnose, and does not assess you. Always follow your own provider.
